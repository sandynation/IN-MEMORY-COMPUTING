About Me

I am a PhD candidate at NIT Agartala with a strong foundation in machine learning systems, hardware-software co-design, distributed computing, and AI accelerator architectures. My research focuses on leveraging machine learning to optimize hardware performance and designing efficient systems for large-scale AI workloads.

With hands-on experience in Python, C/C++, and hardware description languages, I have contributed to research in reinforcement learning-based hardware optimization and FinFET memory systems. I am particularly interested in the intersection of AI infrastructure and hardware design—building systems that efficiently execute modern machine learning workloads.

My goal is to contribute to the advancement of AI/ML infrastructure at scale, bridging the gap between algorithmic innovation and hardware efficiency.
